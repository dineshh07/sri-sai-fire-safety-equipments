HOW TO ADD YOUR REAL IMAGES
Put image files in these folders with EXACTLY these names. Use .webp, .jpg or .png (webp is fastest).
Any image you do not add keeps its placeholder.

images/hero.png            transparent PNG of a fire extinguisher (shows in hero)
images/intro.webp          company intro photo (portrait 4:5 works best)

images/products/01.webp ... 15.webp   in this order:
01 Fire Extinguishers        02 Fire Alarm Systems       03 Fire Hydrant Systems
04 Fire Sprinkler Systems    05 Fire Detection Systems   06 Emergency Lights
07 Fire Safety Signages      08 PPE                      09 Industrial Safety Shoes
10 Safety Gloves             11 Smoke Detectors          12 Fire Buckets and Accessories
13 Fire Suppression Systems  14 Hose Reels and Nozzles    15 First Aid Kits
(landscape 4:3, around 1200px wide)

images/industries/1.webp ... 9.webp   in this order:
1 Manufacturing  2 Commercial  3 Hospitals  4 Education  5 Hotels
6 Warehouses     7 Construction  8 Residential  9 Offices

images/projects/1.webp ... 3.webp     real project photos (portrait 3:4)

Tip: keep each image under 300 KB (squoosh.app can compress to WebP).
